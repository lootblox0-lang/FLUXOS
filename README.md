# FLUXOS

Instead of jumping between a dozen different tools, Fluxos brings your academic schedule, personal life, habits, fitness goals, and wellness routines into one fully customizable dashboard—powered by gamification, hidden achievements, and an offline-first architecture.

Key Highlights:

🎯 Customizable Dashboard: Customize the interface in whatever way you want, full accessibility

⚡ Gamified System: Earn EXP by completing tasks, building habit streaks, and hitting PRs to unlock hidden achievements and arcade access.

🏋️ Sports & Gym Tracking: Dedicated logging for strength progression, sprint/running PRs, and athletic performance.

📚 Student Hub: Dynamic timetable, task manager, and lightweight budget tracker built for daily student life.

🧘 Mindfulness & Focus: Built-in Pomodoro timers and guided breathing exercises to wind down after study sprints.

🕹️ The Arcade: Spend earned EXP/points on quick retro mini-games during study breaks.

🔒 Local-First & Private: Your data stays strictly on your device, with seamless local cross-device sync.
A modular, local-first, gamified productivity workspace for students. Track habits, workouts, budgets, and tasks while earning EXP and unlocking secrets.
